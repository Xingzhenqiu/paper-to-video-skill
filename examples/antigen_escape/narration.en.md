Why can cancer escape a targeted treatment?

Figure one A contrasts a target-positive cancer cell with a target-negative cell.

The car binding domain recognises a particular target antigen.

If cancer cells lose that target, recognition by a car directed only at it can fail.

This resistance mechanism is called antigen escape.

Targeting more than one antigen is one strategy discussed here, not a guarantee against relapse.

This is a qualitative schematic from a two thousand twenty-one review, not a measured treatment comparison.
