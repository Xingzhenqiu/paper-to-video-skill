# Community and collaboration

## Give feedback with minimal material

Use the [issue forms](https://github.com/Xingzhenqiu/paper-to-video-skill/issues/new/choose), or open a short plain issue. English and Chinese are welcome. For bugs, share the command, expected and observed behavior, operating system, Python version and relevant package versions. A public DOI, small excerpt you may share or synthetic example is enough to start; uploading a full paper is not required.

GitHub issues are public. Remove credentials, identifying file paths and confidential content before posting. Do not submit private papers or voice references. The forms ask for no email address, telephone number or automatic usage data.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for four small ways to help and checks for code changes. No project citation, star, watermark, donation or usage reporting is required.

## Optional project credit

You may use this acknowledgement in a video description, slide or project page:

> Created with Paper to Video Skill: https://github.com/Xingzhenqiu/paper-to-video-skill

This acknowledgement is optional. The [software license](../LICENSE) and [source/asset terms](../RIGHTS.md) still apply to redistribution and publication.

## Share a case on your terms

The case form asks you to confirm that you voluntarily make the submitted material public and have permission to share it, including figures, video and voice material. A software license does not grant rights to paper figures, recordings or other third-party assets.

The separate showcase choice defaults to **No: feedback only**. Maintainers may include a case only after an explicit **Yes**. That consent covers the listed public links, a short case summary and any public credit you choose. It does not authorize copying, adapting or relicensing linked assets; those uses need their own permission or applicable license. Declining showcase inclusion does not affect feedback or contributions.

Describe the source, reuse terms, workflow, reproduction steps and completed versus pending scientific, audio and visual checks. Mark synthetic examples. A successful build is not scientific approval, and inclusion is not an endorsement or performance guarantee.

You can later ask in the issue to withdraw showcase consent. Maintainers should remove the case from future versions they control; copies already obtained by others may remain available. Sharing a case does not automatically publish it in a showcase.

## Discuss collaboration

The collaboration form can start a discussion about research-video customization or local deployment and workflow training. Begin with a public description of the goal, audience, desired output and constraints. Do not upload confidential material to the issue.

The form creates an inquiry only. Availability, deliverables, any price, confidentiality arrangements and schedule require separate agreement before work begins. It does not promise a response time, SLA, scientific sign-off or an already launched paid product. No payment or private document submission is requested through these forms.
